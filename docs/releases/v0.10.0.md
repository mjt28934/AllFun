# v0.10.0 — focused updates, a real player, stream providers

This release trims the update screen to the one thing it is for, fills out Settings with
the controls people actually change, and rebuilds the search, player, downloads queue and
tab transitions around motion instead of static screens. Movie details now list the addons
that can actually stream a title.

## What changed

### App updates

- **One version, one button.** Settings → App Updates now shows the newer version and its
  notes with Download / Install. The versions table, install report, automatic-check
  switches and the "how updates work" essay are gone; an up-to-date or failed check is a
  single short card.

### Settings

- **Appearance**: AMOLED black in addition to the existing theme and accent choices.
- **Library**: compact, comfortable or large grid, applied to the Library shelf.
- **Reading**: keep the screen on while a chapter is open, for both readers.
- **Playback**: skip amount (5, 10 or 30 seconds) and auto-play the next episode.
- **Downloads**: the queue is now a full Settings page rather than a shortcut, with the
  existing Wi-Fi-only, concurrency and pause controls.

### Library

- **The Continue row is gone.** The Library opens on the grid; unfinished titles are still
  resumed from their detail pages and the category shelves.

### Search

- **Search is an overlay, not a route.** Tapping the search icon blurs and dims the category
  grid, then fades, slides and scales a panel in over it. Results use the same grid as the
  category, Back returns to the exact scroll position, and the panel closes without
  rebuilding the screen. A "Search every category instead" action still opens the full
  cross-category search.

### Extensions

- **Install Stremio addons from Discover.** The Video scope has a direct card and dialog for
  pasting an addon URL; installed addons and Jellyfin servers are grouped underneath with
  their status.

### Motion

- **Tabs move like a pager.** Switching between Movies, Books, Manga, Library and Settings
  fades and slides in the direction of travel over 220 ms; back navigation keeps its own
  mirrored motion. Reduce motion still removes all of it.

### Player

- **Compose controls instead of the stock controller.** Tap toggles an auto-hiding top bar
  (back, title, next episode) and bottom bar (time, scrubber, source picker, next episode).
  The centre row has rewind, play/pause and forward, labelled with the configured skip
  amount. Double-tap the left or right half to seek. Stream picking, resume, subtitles,
  torrent progress and readable errors are unchanged.

### Movie details

- **Stream providers, not catalogue addons.** For a Stremio title, the source section lists
  only addons that declare `stream` and accept that id (Torrentio, Comet and similar), and
  selecting one records a playback preference. Catalogue-only addons keep supplying covers
  and metadata but no longer appear as if they could play the film. The player tries the
  preferred addon's releases first and still offers every other stream.

### Downloads

- **Komikku-style queue.** Sticky title headers with a drag handle, item-count badge, one-line
  summary and a hairline progress bar; compact rows with percentage, status and a thin
  progress line; the floating Pause / Resume button stays. Every menu action and confirmation
  from 0.9.x is unchanged.

## Breaking changes

- None. Library, history, extensions/providers, downloads, backups and integrations are
  untouched; the database schema does not change in this release.
