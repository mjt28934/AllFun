# v0.13.1 — Liquid Glass refinement

A visual follow-up to 0.13.0. The interface, features, routes, settings keys and data are
unchanged; the glass itself now behaves like a lens instead of a tinted card, and the bottom
sheet no longer runs past the display.

## What changed

### The material

- **The lens samples where the glass is.** Every surface reads the screen's world field and
  its own position, so a panel shows the part of the backdrop it actually covers, magnified
  about its centre. Sliding a control across the screen changes what it refracts.
- **A real edge bend.** A concentric ring around the outline re-samples the field at a harder
  zoom before the body tint. The rim bends light, and a tinted control stays tinted all the
  way to its edge instead of growing a clear halo.
- **A vertical specular rim.** The 1.8dp rim is a top-bright, bottom-dim vertical gradient
  rather than a sweep, so the highlight behaves like light from above on wide and narrow
  controls alike.
- **Thickness and tint.** A shade under the lit top edge and over the bottom half reads as
  glass slab depth. `GlassSurface` gained `tint`/`tintAlpha`; the primary action is now a
  tinted clear capsule rather than a flat accent pill.
- **More transparent glass, larger radii.** Clear 0.18, frosted 0.48, AMOLED 0.55, light
  0.36; panel 26, sheet 34, menu 24, input 20, poster 10. The rim, thickness shades and grain
  carry legibility while the world shows through.
- **A world worth bending.** A cool top light, a diagonal shaft and a vignette give the lens
  structure to displace.
- **Selected states are light, not outlines.** The switch track is near-solid accent when
  checked, the segmented control's pill is neutral white glass, and the nav blob is a soft
  accent/white gradient with no border.

### Sheets and dialogs

- The sheet window now covers the real display (`FLAG_LAYOUT_IN_SCREEN`, no fitted insets,
  top-start gravity), so the last row sits on the navigation bar instead of 136px below the
  screen.
- Sheets cap at `maxHeight - 56dp`, scroll their content, and pad for the navigation bar; the
  scrim is lighter (32%) and the world stays visible behind it. Dialogs share the window
  setup, so their centring is no longer shifted by the status bar.

## Honest limits

- The lens bends the deterministic world field, not the posters or artwork scrolling under a
  panel. Refracting live content needs a GPU backdrop shader; that remains out of scope.
- No per-frame blur, and no change to any screen's logic, data, routes or settings.

## Breaking changes

- None. Same database schema, preferences, providers, addons, downloads, backups and routes.
